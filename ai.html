AI 平台搭建Building the AI Platform

目标：搭建独立的本地 AI 平台——能完全自主运营一家正常医药研发公司所需全部职能的 AI 体系。下方关系网是体系全貌，再往下是把它搭起来所需要的五层底座。 The goal: an independent, on-premise AI platform that can autonomously operate every function a normal drug R&D company needs. Below: the system network, then the five layers required to build it.

体系关系网 · 一家公司需要的全部职能THE NETWORK · EVERY FUNCTION OF A COMPANY

本地 AI 中枢统一调度八大职能模块：研发、临床、注册、生产质量、药物警戒构成药物生命周期主链路（虚线环），项目管理与商务法务、财务人力构成经营保障面。橙色角标为起步模块：项目管理 AI 与临床开发 AI（按 protocol 自动生成各阶段表格内容）。 A local AI core orchestrates eight function modules: discovery, clinical, regulatory, CMC/quality and pharmacovigilance form the drug-lifecycle loop (dashed ring), while project management, BD/legal and finance/HR form the business backbone. First modules: PM AI + clinical-dev AI — automate my own daily work first, then expand along the chain.

搭建底座 · 把它搭起来需要什么THE FOUNDATION · WHAT IT TAKES

独立的本地平台意味着模型与数据都掌握在自己手里——这对医药研发的保密与合规是刚需。五层底座缺一不可。 "Independent and local" means models and data stay in your own hands — a hard requirement for confidentiality and compliance in drug R&D. All five layers are indispensable.

L1

硬件层 · 本地算力Hardware

本地 GPU 服务器与私有部署：模型与数据全部留在内网，满足保密与合规On-prem GPU servers; models and data stay inside the firewall for confidentiality

L2

模型层 · 大模型Models

开源大模型为底座，医药语料微调 + RAG 检索增强，按职能配置专才模型Open-source LLMs fine-tuned on pharma corpora, plus RAG; specialist models per function

L3

数据层 · 知识底座Data

SOP 库 · protocol 模板 · 历史项目数据 · 法规库统一入库，向量化可检索SOPs, protocol templates, historical project data and regulations, vectorized & searchable

L4

接口层 · 系统对接Integration

对接 LIMS / CTMS / eCTD / ERP 等业务系统，让 AI 能读数、能办事Connects LIMS, CTMS, eCTD and ERP so the AI can read data and take action

L5

治理层 · 人在回路Governance

关键决策人工复核，审计追踪、权限分级与容灾备份，符合 GxP 要求Human review for key decisions; audit trails, access tiers and backups, GxP-aligned

终态：中枢统一调度八大职能模块，独立、自主地运营一家具备完整职能的医药研发公司——而我站在中枢之上，做最终的判断与决策。 End state: the core orchestrates all eight function modules, independently running a fully functional drug R&D company — with me standing above the core, making the final judgments and decisions.
