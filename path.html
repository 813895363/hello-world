机构关系图 · 谁在药物研发中扮演什么角色STAKEHOLDER MAP

一个药物从发现到上市，从来不是一家机构完成的：申办方（药企）居中统筹，上游对接科研机构与 CDMO，下游组织 CRO、研究中心与中心实验室执行，全程接受监管机构审评监督。 No single organization takes a drug from discovery to market: the sponsor orchestrates, with academia and CDMOs upstream, CROs / sites / central labs executing downstream, and regulators reviewing throughout.

上游：科研成果经授权转化给药企，CDMO 承担工艺开发与生产；下游：药企委托 CRO 运营临床试验，研究中心入组受试者并采样，中心实验室完成检测，数据回流申办方；监管机构全程审评监督。 Upstream: academic findings are licensed to sponsors, CDMOs handle process development and manufacturing; downstream: sponsors contract CROs to run trials, sites enroll and sample patients, central labs test and return data; regulators review throughout.
