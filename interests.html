感兴趣的研究方向Research Interests

以"永生（Immortality）"相关的分子生物学为长期目标，沿三条主线积累：端粒与基因组稳定性 · 蛋白质设计与编辑 · 肿瘤机制与靶向防治 A long-term focus on the molecular biology of immortality, built along three lines: telomere & genome stability · protein design & engineering · cancer mechanisms & targeted prevention

总览OVERVIEW

我相信，基因组的不完整性与基因突变的调控在普通细胞与癌细胞中存在根本差异，而基因治疗是通往"永生"的可行路径——病毒载体与长寿相关蛋白设计都离不开蛋白质编辑技术。因此我沿三条主线积累：理解端粒结构稳定性与染色体断裂修复如何维持基因组完整性；掌握蛋白质设计与高通量筛选，以改造功能蛋白的募集效率与结合亲和力；深入肿瘤发生机制与靶向防治策略。以下每位研究者的工作都对应我关注的具体方向，并附代表性论文与我的解读。 I believe the incompleteness of the genome and the regulation of gene mutations differ fundamentally between normal cells and cancer cells, and that gene therapy is the viable path toward immortality — both viral vectors and immortality-related protein design require mastery of protein engineering. I therefore build along three lines: how telomere structural stability and chromosome-break repair preserve genome integrity; protein design and high-throughput screening to tune recruitment efficiency and binding affinity; and tumor initiation mechanisms with targeted prevention. Each researcher below maps to a specific direction, with representative papers and my notes.

端粒与基因组稳定性TELOMERE & GENOME STABILITY

Dr. Jack Griffith

T-loop · R-loop · TERRA

研究端粒 T-loop 与 R-loop 形成的结构与分子机制，以及它们可能导致的基因组不稳定性。 The structure and molecular mechanisms of telomere T-loop and R-loop formation, and the potential genomic instability they cause.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 帮助我深入理解端粒的结构稳定性机制，并从 R-loop 相关酶的调控中获得启发，探索实现端粒酶持续合成能力（telomerase processivity）的路径。 Deepens my understanding of telomere structural stability and inspires me, through the regulation of R-loop-related enzymes, toward achieving telomerase processivity.

代表论文PAPER
Formation of T-Loops at the Telomere: A Link Between Looping and Transcription
Frontiers in Genetics, 2019

系统梳理端粒 T-loop 的形成机制及其与端粒转录产物 TERRA 的联系——TERRA 在端粒处形成的 R-loop 正是我关注的端粒稳定性调控切入点。 Dissects how telomeric T-loops form and how they connect to telomeric transcription (TERRA) — the TERRA R-loops at the heart of my interest in telomere stability regulation.

Dr. Dale Ramsden

Pol θ · TMEJ

研究 DNA 修复酶 Pol θ（DNA 聚合酶 θ）介导的染色体断裂修复过程。 The DNA-repair polymerase Pol θ and the repair of chromosome breaks.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 帮助我理解染色体断裂的修复过程，启发我思考细胞如何维持基因组完整性。 Helps me understand how chromosome breaks are repaired and how cells maintain genome integrity.

代表论文PAPER
Mechanistic basis for microhomology identification and genome scarring by polymerase theta
PNAS, 2020

阐明 Pol θ 介导的末端连接（TMEJ）如何从 3' 端扫描识别微同源序列、完成断端修复，以及其"模板化插入"如何在 BRCA1/2 突变肿瘤中留下特征性基因瘢痕——为针对同源重组缺陷型癌症的治疗策略提供机制依据。 Shows how Pol θ-mediated end joining (TMEJ) scans from the 3′ terminus to find microhomologies, and how its "templated insertions" leave characteristic genomic scars in BRCA1/2-mutant tumors — mechanistic rationale for targeting HR-deficient cancers.

Dr. Steve Henikoff

CUT&RUN · CUT&Tag

开发基于蛋白质-DNA 与 RNA-DNA 结合的染色质研究遗传工具。 Development of genetic tools for chromatin study through protein-DNA and RNA-DNA binding.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 正契合我的兴趣——以工具开发解析端粒延伸相关转录因子及其结合的靶基因序列。 Aligns with my interest in building tools to understand telomere-elongation-related transcription factors and the target gene sequences they bind.

代表论文PAPER
An efficient targeted nuclease strategy for high-resolution mapping of DNA binding sites (CUT&RUN)
eLife, 2017 · Skene & Henikoff

CUT&RUN 以抗体靶向 MNase 原位释放蛋白-DNA 复合物，背景极低、分辨率高，测序量仅需 ChIP 的约 1/10——可直接用于绘制端粒相关转录因子的全基因组结合图谱。 CUT&RUN uses antibody-targeted MNase to release protein-DNA complexes in situ — ultra-low background, high resolution, ~1/10 the sequencing depth of ChIP; directly applicable to mapping telomere-related transcription factors genome-wide.

代表论文PAPER
CUT&Tag for efficient epigenomic profiling of small samples and single cells
Nature Communications, 2019 · Kaya-Okur et al.

CUT&Tag 用 Tn5 转座酶替换 MNase，把建库流程压缩到单细胞尺度——适合稀有样本（如早期肿瘤克隆）的表观基因组分析。 CUT&Tag swaps MNase for Tn5 transposase, compressing library prep to the single-cell scale — suited to epigenomic analysis of rare samples such as early tumor clones.

Dr. Gerald Smith

减数分裂 DSB 修复Meiotic DSB Repair

研究减数分裂过程中 DNA 双链断裂（DSB）形成与修复的分子机制。 Molecular mechanisms of DNA double-strand break formation and repair during meiosis.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 为我理解癌症相关非整倍体（aneuploidy）的形成与预防提供全新视角。 Provides me with a new perspective on the formation and prevention of cancer-induced aneuploidy.

代表论文PAPER
DNA intermediates of meiotic recombination in synchronous S. pombe at optimal temperature
Nucleic Acids Research, 2014 · Hyppa et al.

在同步化裂殖酵母中直接物理检测减数分裂重组的 DNA 中间体，精确刻画从 DSB 形成到修复完成的时序——染色体断裂-修复失衡正是非整倍体的根源。 Directly detects DNA intermediates of meiotic recombination in synchronized S. pombe, resolving the timeline from DSB formation to repair — the break-repair imbalance at the root of aneuploidy.

蛋白质设计与编辑PROTEIN DESIGN & ENGINEERING

Dr. Brian Kuhlman

计算蛋白设计 · 免疫治疗Computational Design · Immunotherapy

计算蛋白质设计：设计稳定蛋白，并已成功应用于癌症免疫治疗（相关工作与 2024 年诺贝尔化学奖相关联）。 Computational protein design: stable proteins, successfully applied to cancer immunotherapy (work connected to the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry).

与我的关联：Why it matters: 我认为基因治疗是通往"永生"的路径，而病毒载体与长寿相关蛋白设计都必须掌握蛋白质编辑技术——其实验室正是"设计稳定蛋白并推向治疗应用"的直接范本。 Gene therapy is my path toward immortality, and both viral vectors and immortality-related protein design require protein engineering — his lab is the direct model of "designing stable proteins into therapeutic applications."

代表论文PAPER
De novo design of stable proteins that efficaciously inhibit oncogenic G proteins
Science Advances, 2023

从头设计出可高效抑制致癌 G 蛋白的稳定蛋白，展示了计算设计直接转化为治疗候选分子的能力——正是改造长寿相关蛋白所需的技术路线。 De novo-designed stable proteins that potently inhibit oncogenic G proteins — proof that computational design converts directly into therapeutic candidates, the very pipeline needed to engineer immortality-related proteins.

Dr. Douglas Fowler

DMS · Variant Effect Maps

针对特定功能的蛋白质设计与大规模筛选（深度突变扫描 / 变异效应图谱）。 Design and screening of proteins for specific functions (deep mutational scanning / variant effect maps).

与我的关联：Why it matters: 提升我改造"永生研究相关蛋白"的募集效率（recruitment efficiency）与结合亲和力（binding affinity）的知识储备。 Strengthens my ability to tune the recruitment efficiency and binding affinity of my immortality-research-related proteins.

代表论文PAPER
Deep mutational scanning: a new style of protein science
Nature Methods, 2014 · Fowler & Fields

DMS 通过"饱和突变文库 + 功能选择 + 测序定量"系统测定每个氨基酸替换对蛋白功能的影响——为定向优化结合亲和力与稳定性提供高通量数据引擎。 DMS couples saturated mutagenesis libraries with functional selection and sequencing to score every amino-acid substitution — a high-throughput data engine for optimizing binding affinity and stability.

Dr. Kalogriopoulos

合成 GPCR · 可编程细胞Synthetic GPCR · Programmable Cells

工程化的合成可编程 GPCR 蛋白系统，按需调控特定细胞信号。 Engineering synthetic, programmable GPCR-based protein systems that regulate specific signals.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 帮助我积累用于药物研发与免疫治疗的蛋白质工程知识。 Helps me gain protein-engineering knowledge for drug discovery and immunotherapy.

代表论文PAPER
Synthetic GPCRs for programmable sensing and control of cell behaviour (PAGERs)
Nature, 2025 · Kalogriopoulos et al.

PAGER 以纳米抗体"门控"GPCR 支架：抗原结合即解除自抑制，可响应十余种可溶性/细胞表面抗原，驱动转基因表达或内源信号——已展示控制 T 细胞迁移、诱导巨噬细胞分化、驱动治疗性抗体分泌等细胞治疗级应用。 PAGER gates GPCR scaffolds with a nanobody: antigen binding relieves auto-inhibition, responding to a dozen+ soluble or surface antigens to drive transgene expression or endogenous signaling — demonstrated for T-cell migration control, macrophage differentiation, and therapeutic antibody secretion.

肿瘤机制与靶向防治CANCER MECHANISMS & TARGETING

Dr. Jun Dai

Haspin · 皮肤癌靶向Skin Cancer Targeting

研究皮肤病（尤其是皮肤癌）的治疗策略，包括 haspin 等有丝分裂激酶在细胞周期进程中的作用。 Therapies for skin diseases, especially skin cancer — including the roles of mitotic kinases such as haspin in cell cycle progression.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 帮助我建立在肿瘤靶向与预防方向的知识与技能体系。 Builds my knowledge and skills in cancer targeting and prevention.

代表论文PAPER
Anti-Melanoma Activities of Haspin Inhibitor CHR-6494 Deployed as a Single Agent or in a Synergistic Combination with MEK Inhibitor
Journal of Cancer, 2017 · Han et al.（Dai J. 通讯作者）

证明 haspin 抑制剂 CHR-6494 单药即可剂量依赖地抑制黑色素瘤生长并促进凋亡，与 MEK 抑制剂联用呈协同效应——有丝分裂激酶作为肿瘤治疗靶点的直接证据。 Shows the haspin inhibitor CHR-6494 dose-dependently suppresses melanoma growth and induces apoptosis, synergizing with MEK inhibition — direct evidence for mitotic kinases as anti-cancer targets.

Dr. Collier

SB 转座子 · 致癌基因筛选Oncogene Screens

利用工程化 SB（Sleeping Beauty）转座子在肿瘤中鉴定致癌基因。 Using engineered Sleeping Beauty (SB) transposons to identify oncogenes in tumors.

与我的关联：Why it matters: 为我提供体内（in vivo）遗传筛选的实验经验，揭示肿瘤起始与进展的机制。 Gives me in vivo genetic-screen experience that reveals mechanisms of tumor initiation and progression.

代表论文PAPER
Cancer gene discovery in solid tumours using transposon-based somatic mutagenesis in the mouse
Nature, 2005 · Collier et al.

将 T2/Onc 转座子设计为"致癌基因陷阱"，在小鼠体内随机插入以激活癌基因或失活抑癌基因，开创了实体瘤致癌基因体内筛选的范式。 Engineered the T2/Onc transposon as an "oncogene trap" that mobilizes in mice to activate oncogenes or disrupt tumor suppressors — the founding paradigm of in vivo solid-tumor cancer-gene screens.
