起点 ｜ 加入实验室The Beginning · Joining the Lab

大三伊始，我加入了 Kristopher McConnell 博士的实验室，开展 Gal4/UAS 系统研究并积累基因相关研究经验，为日后探索"永生"这一课题打下基础。在 McConnell 博士的指导下，我以黑腹果蝇（Drosophila melanogaster）为实验模型研究生殖系统——日常饲养、杂交与种群维护——并逐步具备了执行实验操作、理解 protocol 并独立开展研究的能力。获得季度实验经费后，我正式开始了 Gal4/UAS 系统的研究。 At the beginning of my junior year, I joined Dr. Kristopher McConnell's lab to research the Gal4/UAS system and gain experience in genetic research, which laid the foundation for my future research on immortality. Under Dr. McConnell's supervision, I learned to use Drosophila melanogaster as an experimental model to study the reproductive system — daily care, hybridization, and stock maintenance — while building the ability to perform procedures, understand protocols, and conduct independent research. After receiving quarterly experimental funding, I formally began my Gal4/UAS research.

验证假设 ｜ Hsp70:Gal4 与卵巢细胞死亡Testing the Hypothesis · Hsp70:Gal4 & Ovarian Cell Death

Gal4/UAS 系统可控制下游靶基因的组织特异性表达，被广泛应用于果蝇癌症研究中的分子信号通路与 p53 等致癌基因研究。然而，McConnell 博士此前的研究发现了该系统的缺陷，我的初步实验进一步证实了他的假设："热诱导型 Hsp70:Gal4 的表达会导致果蝇卵巢中卵泡细胞与滋养细胞死亡，并使雌蝇无法产卵。"我设置对照实验，比较对照组与 Hsp70:Gal4 雌蝇组前四天的产卵数量；同时解剖果蝇卵巢、固定卵室、以 DAPI 染色、制作荧光显微镜切片并拍照，分析卵巢形态。Hsp70:Gal4 雌蝇产卵数显著低于对照组，卵泡细胞与滋养细胞的细胞核出现皱缩，共同支持了这一假设。与此同时，我通过 caspase 与 actin-red 染料的荧光图像分析，发现了卵巢形态中的其他异常，并测试了组织特异性 Gal4/UAS 表达对果蝇卵巢相关神经与肌肉组织的影响。 The Gal4/UAS system, which controls tissue-specific expression of downstream target genes, is widely used in Drosophila cancer studies of molecular signaling pathways and oncogenes such as p53. However, Dr. McConnell's earlier work had identified flaws in the system, and my initial experiments further confirmed his hypothesis: "Expression of the heat-inducible Hsp70:Gal4 causes follicle and nurse cell death in the fly ovary and prevents female flies from laying eggs." I set up controlled experiments comparing the number of eggs laid in the first four days between control and Hsp70:Gal4 females. I also dissected Drosophila ovaries, fixed egg chambers, stained cells with DAPI, prepared fluorescence microscopy slides, and photographed them to analyze ovary morphology. The low egg count in Hsp70:Gal4 females versus controls, together with shrinkage of follicle and nurse cell nuclei, supported the hypothesis. At the same time, by analyzing fluorescence images with caspase and actin-red dyes, I identified additional disruptions in ovarian morphology and tested the effects of tissue-specific Gal4/UAS expression on ovary-related nerve and muscle tissue.

追溯机制 ｜ 从上游到下游的排查Tracing the Mechanism · Upstream to Downstream

好奇心让我没有止步于验证最初的假设——我想弄清结果背后的驱动机制。与 McConnell 博士探讨后，我再次获得季度实验经费，开始沿着 Gal4/UAS 系统从上游到下游排查 Gal4 诱导异常的机制：Gal4 启动子、Gal4 蛋白及其转录激活域。 Curiosity kept me from stopping at confirming the initial hypothesis — I wanted to understand what was driving the results. After consulting Dr. McConnell, I received another quarterly grant and began investigating the mechanism of Gal4-induced disruption from upstream to downstream of the Gal4/UAS system: the Gal4 promoter, the Gal4 protein, and its transactivation domain.

首先，我验证了不同 Gal4 启动子是否会引发与 Hsp70:Gal4 类似的异常：针对不同 driver 设置多组对照，比较荧光图像中卵泡细胞与滋养细胞的死亡比例并统计产卵率。结果显示，各实验组均出现不同程度的细胞死亡、产卵数显著少于对照组，提示不同 Gal4 driver 的共表达会增强类似的异常。其次，我使用表达缺失转录激活域 Gal4 蛋白的 Hsp70:Gal4 DBD，以及仅在喂食类固醇孕酮后才能结合 UAS 的 Act:Gal4-PR Switch，检验在不结合下游 UAS 的情况下是否仍会发生细胞死亡与产卵减少——两组均未出现异常，说明该效应依赖于 Gal4 的转录激活功能。第三，我比对并鉴定了若干与果蝇 UAS 同源的基因序列。虽然时间所限未能完成后续实验，但我提出假设："Gal4 蛋白激活了 UAS 同源基因的下游序列，诱导了果蝇体内一条或多条细胞死亡通路。" First, I tested whether different Gal4 promoters cause disruptions similar to Hsp70:Gal4: across multiple control groups with different drivers, I compared follicle- and nurse-cell death ratios in fluorescence images and measured egg-laying rates. All experimental groups showed varying degrees of cell death and markedly fewer eggs than controls, suggesting that co-expression of different Gal4 drivers enhances similar disruptions. Second, I used Hsp70:Gal4 DBD — which expresses Gal4 protein lacking the transactivation domain — and Act:Gal4-PR Switch, whose Gal4 binds UAS only after feeding the steroid progesterone, to determine whether cell death and reduced egg production occur without downstream UAS binding. Neither group showed disruption, indicating the effect depends on Gal4's transcriptional activation function. Third, I compared and identified several gene sequences homologous to the Drosophila UAS sequence. Although time constraints prevented the follow-up experiments, I hypothesized that "Gal4 protein activates downstream sequences of UAS-homologous genes, inducing one or more cell-death pathways in Drosophila."

挑战与收获 ｜ 两次学术展示Challenges & Outcomes · Two Showcases

研究过程并非一帆风顺：部分批次数据不稳定、环境因素影响导致需要重复实验、蛋白相互作用的知识储备不足。通过增加实验次数、持续优化实验方案、向导师请教并不断补足所需知识，我稳步推进了研究。这些工作加深了我对 Gal4/UAS 分子通路的理解，也让我看到它对使用该基因工具的其他研究的潜在影响；研究成果先后在 2022 年 "Research in the Rotunda" 和 2023 年第 64 届果蝇研究年会（64th Annual Drosophila Research Conference）上展示。 The work came with challenges — unstable data in some trials, experiments repeated due to environmental factors, and limited prior knowledge of protein interactions. By increasing trial counts, continuously refining my approach, consulting my mentor, and steadily acquiring the knowledge I needed, I kept the research moving forward. These efforts deepened my understanding of the Gal4/UAS molecular pathway and its potential impact on other studies using this genetic tool, and led to my work being exhibited at "Research in the Rotunda" in 2022 and the "64th Annual Drosophila Research Conference" in 2023.

毒理检测与体内研究Toxicology & In Vivo Research

毕业后，我接受 offer 以 Study Technician 身份加入 LabCorp，为多家制药与生物科技公司开展 FDA 批准前的毒理检测——其中许多是肿瘤药物。在研究中，我负责样本采集、受试物给药与体内模型的数据分析，涉及小鼠、大鼠、灵长类与犬等模型，重点关注针对胰腺癌与肺腺癌的潜在疗法。 After graduation, I accepted an offer as a Study Technician at LabCorp, conducting toxicity testing for pharmaceutical and biotech companies ahead of FDA approval — many of them oncology drugs. Within these studies I was responsible for sample collection, administration of test articles, and data analysis across in vivo models including mice, rats, primates, and canines, with a focus on potential therapies for pancreatic cancer and lung adenocarcinoma.

多项目并行运营 ｜ 年均 7+ 研究Multi-Project Operations · 7+ Studies/Year

在 LabCorp 的三年间，我平均每年并行支持至少 7 个 preclinical / early-stage 研究项目，累计参与约 40 个研究，其中包括 Cell & Gene Therapy 等高复杂度项目。我对接 Study Director 与跨职能团队，协助研究启动、执行监控与关键节点推进；同时承担实验室日常运行支持——实验排期协调、资源使用支持与关键节点保障——在药理、毒理、生物分析等多职能团队之间保障研究沿时间线推进。 Over three years at LabCorp, I supported 7+ concurrent preclinical / early-stage studies per year — roughly 40 studies in total — including highly complex Cell & Gene Therapy programs. I coordinated with Study Directors and cross-functional teams on study initiation, execution monitoring, and milestone delivery, while supporting day-to-day lab operations: scheduling coordination, resource allocation, and critical-path coverage — keeping studies on timeline across pharmacology, toxicology, and bioanalysis functions.

质量与偏差管理 ｜ 关键节点决策Quality & Deviation Management · Critical-Point Decisions

我持续跟踪 study 执行进度，及时识别 protocol / SOP 偏差并向 Study Director 汇报，并参与研究文件与数据的 QC 审核，主动发现并解决问题，从源头降低偏差发生风险。在多物种非临床研究项目中，我负责关键实验节点的执行决策与质量把控，确保研究按方案及 SOP 要求顺利推进。 I tracked study execution continuously, identified protocol / SOP deviations and reported them to Study Directors, and performed QC review of study documents and data — proactively resolving issues to reduce deviation risk at the source. In multi-species nonclinical programs, I owned execution decisions and quality control at critical study milestones, ensuring studies advanced in full compliance with protocols and SOPs.

独立当班 ｜ 高压下的现场管理Independent Shift Leadership · Decisions Under Pressure

在公司业务量最大的部门（Client），我长期承担 second shift 工作安排。在监管资源有限的班次里，我独立处理实验异常与紧急事件——包括作为 certified technician，在兽医或 SD 不在场时执行经授权的人道处置（humane sacrifice）——保障实验连续性、动物福利与研究合规。这段经历锻炼了我在高压环境下快速评估风险、独立决策并兜底全局的能力。 In the company's highest-volume department (Client), I worked second shift long-term. With limited supervisory resources on shift, I independently handled experimental anomalies and emergencies — including, as a certified technician, performing authorized humane endpoints when the veterinarian or SD was off-site — safeguarding study continuity, animal welfare, and compliance. This forged my ability to assess risk quickly, make independent decisions, and own the outcome under pressure.

成长与方向Growth & Direction

这段经历把我塑造成一名严谨而沉着的研究者：能沉着应对紧急情况、高效沟通，并在多任务并行中主动承担主导角色。同时，在细胞与基因治疗实验室的工作以及学术会议经历，让我接触到药物递送与药物设计的前沿技术，点燃了我对药物研发的兴趣，也让我认识到基因编辑与蛋白质工程是未来医疗——也是实现我个人人生目标——的关键路径。 This experience shaped me into a responsible and calm researcher — able to handle emergencies, communicate effectively, and take the lead while multitasking. Meanwhile, working in cell and gene therapy labs and attending conferences exposed me to cutting-edge drug delivery and design technologies, sparked my interest in drug development, and made me realize that gene editing and protein engineering are essential approaches for the future of medicine — and for achieving my own life goals.
