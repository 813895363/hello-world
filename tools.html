基因组与染色质工具GENOME & CHROMATIN TOOLS

CUT&RUN / CUT&Tag

Henikoff · eLife 2017 / Nat Commun 2019

抗体在完整细胞核内原位识别目标蛋白，引导 MNase（CUT&RUN）或 Tn5 转座酶（CUT&Tag）仅在其结合位点附近切割 DNA，释放的片段直接建库测序——无需交联与超声破碎。 An antibody finds the target protein in intact nuclei and guides MNase (CUT&RUN) or Tn5 transposase (CUT&Tag) to cut DNA only near its binding sites; released fragments go straight to library prep — no crosslinking or sonication.

领域运用：Applications: 背景比 ChIP-seq 低一个数量级、测序量仅需约 1/10，可做到单细胞；已广泛用于转录因子定位、组蛋白修饰图谱与临床微量样本。对我的意义：直接绘制端粒延伸相关转录因子在基因组上的结合位点。 An order of magnitude lower background than ChIP-seq at ~1/10 the reads, down to single cells; standard for transcription-factor mapping, histone-modification profiling, and scarce clinical samples. For me: mapping where telomere-elongation factors bind the genome.

T-loop 电镜成像T-loop Electron Microscopy

Griffith · Front Genet 2019

将纯化的端粒 DNA 直接置于电子显微镜下观察：端粒末端回折形成套索状的 T-loop，3' 单链尾侵入双链区形成 D-loop——把"端粒如何自我保护"变成可以直接看见的结构。 Purified telomeric DNA goes straight under the electron microscope: the chromosome end folds back into a lariat-shaped T-loop, with the 3′ single-stranded tail invading the duplex to form a D-loop — making "how telomeres protect themselves" directly visible.

领域运用：Applications: 端粒结构生物学与基因组稳定性评估的金标准成像手段；配合基因敲除可定量 shelterin 各组分对环形成的贡献，也用于观察 TERRA R-loop 等异常结构。 The gold-standard imaging readout for telomere structural biology and genome stability; with knockouts it quantifies each shelterin component's contribution to loop formation, and reveals aberrant structures such as TERRA R-loops.

减数分裂 DSB 中间体分析Meiotic DSB Intermediate Assays

Smith · NAR 2014

在同步化减数分裂的裂殖酵母中定时取样，用 Southern blot / 物理检测直接捕捉重组 DNA 中间体（断裂分子、单链入侵、联合分子），把"断裂→修复"的时序精确到分钟级。 Time-course sampling of synchronized meiotic S. pombe with Southern-blot / physical detection of recombination intermediates (broken molecules, strand-invasion products, joint molecules) resolves the break-to-repair timeline at minute resolution.

领域运用：Applications: 减数分裂重组机制研究的核心方法；解释交叉形成失败如何导致非整倍体配子——肿瘤非整倍体与生殖不育的共同源头。 A core method of meiotic recombination research; explains how failed crossover formation yields aneuploid gametes — the shared root of tumor aneuploidy and reproductive infertility.

Pol θ / TMEJ 修复分析Repair Analysis

Ramsden · PNAS 2020

Pol θ 的解旋酶结构域把两条断裂端拉近，聚合酶结构域从 3' 端扫描并配对 2–6 bp 微同源序列，再跨越缺口合成 DNA——θ 介导末端连接（TMEJ）因此总会留下插入或缺失的"基因瘢痕"。 Pol θ's helicase domain synapses two broken ends while its polymerase scans from the 3′ terminus for 2–6 bp microhomologies, then synthesizes across the gap — θ-mediated end joining (TMEJ) therefore always leaves insertion/deletion "genomic scars."

领域运用：Applications: TMEJ 瘢痕是 HRD（同源重组缺陷）肿瘤的特征指纹，可用于分子分型；Pol θ 抑制剂与 PARP 抑制剂构成合成致死组合，是当前肿瘤药物研发热点。 TMEJ scars are the fingerprint of HRD (homologous-recombination-deficient) tumors for molecular subtyping; Pol θ inhibitors form synthetic-lethal combinations with PARP inhibitors — a hot area of oncology drug development.

蛋白工程工具PROTEIN ENGINEERING TOOLS

计算蛋白从头设计Computational De Novo Design

Kuhlman · Sci Adv 2023

从目标功能出发反向设计：先搭建理想化的蛋白骨架，再用 Rosetta 等能量函数搜索能稳定折叠成该骨架的氨基酸序列，体外表达验证——不依赖任何天然模板。 Design backwards from function: build an idealized backbone, then let energy functions (e.g. Rosetta) search for sequences that fold stably into it, and verify by expression — no natural template required.

领域运用：Applications: 治疗性结合蛋白（如抑制致癌 G 蛋白的迷你结合剂）、酶工程、疫苗抗原与合成生物学元件；2024 年诺贝尔化学奖已认可这一范式。对我的意义：改造长寿相关蛋白的核心生产力。 Therapeutic binders (e.g. minibinders against oncogenic G proteins), enzyme engineering, vaccine antigens, and synthetic-biology parts — a paradigm recognized by the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. For me: the core engine for engineering immortality-related proteins.

深度突变扫描（DMS）Deep Mutational Scanning

Fowler · Nat Methods 2014

对目标蛋白做饱和突变（每个位点 × 20 种氨基酸），混合文库经功能选择（结合、活性、稳定性）后高通量测序，用突变体频率变化给每个替换打分——一次实验测完上万种变异。 Saturate every position with all 20 amino acids, pass the pooled library through a functional selection (binding, activity, stability), then score each substitution by its frequency change in deep sequencing — tens of thousands of variants in one experiment.

领域运用：Applications: 绘制"变异效应图谱"指导亲和力/稳定性定向优化；临床上用于解读意义未明变异（VUS）；也是训练蛋白语言模型的黄金数据集。对我的意义：系统化改造蛋白募集效率与结合亲和力的数据引擎。 Variant effect maps guide directed affinity/stability optimization; clinically they interpret variants of uncertain significance (VUS); they are also gold-standard training data for protein language models. For me: the data engine to systematically tune recruitment efficiency and binding affinity.

PAGER 合成 GPCRSynthetic GPCR

Kalogriopoulos · Nature 2025

把纳米抗体"闸门"装进 GPCR 跨膜支架：没有抗原时受体自抑制；抗原结合纳米抗体后构象解锁，激活下游 G 蛋白信号或转基因表达——把"识别某种抗原"自由接线到"执行某种细胞行为"。 A nanobody "gate" is built into a GPCR scaffold: auto-inhibited without antigen; antigen binding unlocks the conformation, activating G-protein signaling or transgene expression — freely wiring "sense an antigen" to "execute a cell behavior."

领域运用：Applications: 可编程细胞治疗（控制 T 细胞迁移、巨噬细胞分化）、抗原响应型药物分泌、活体生物传感器；与 CAR-T 互补的新一代细胞工程平台。 Programmable cell therapy (steering T-cell migration, macrophage differentiation), antigen-responsive drug secretion, and in vivo biosensors — a next-generation cell-engineering platform complementary to CAR-T.

肿瘤研究工具CANCER RESEARCH TOOLS

SB 转座子体内筛选Transposon In Vivo Screen

Collier · Nature 2005

工程化的 Sleeping Beauty 转座子携带强启动子与剪接受体（激活癌基因）以及双向多聚腺苷酸信号（截断抑癌基因），在小鼠体细胞内随机跳跃插入；对肿瘤 DNA 做插入位点测序，即可反推驱动基因。 Engineered Sleeping Beauty transposons carry a strong promoter plus splice acceptor (to activate oncogenes) and bidirectional polyA signals (to truncate tumor suppressors); they hop randomly in mouse somatic cells, and sequencing insertion sites in tumors reveals the drivers.

领域运用：Applications: 实体瘤驱动基因的体内功能基因组学筛选金标准；可定制组织特异性启动，系统绘制肝癌、肠癌等驱动图谱，为靶点发现服务。 The gold standard for in vivo functional-genomic screens of solid-tumor drivers; tissue-specific launching maps driver landscapes of liver, colorectal and other cancers for target discovery.

Haspin 激酶抑制剂Kinase Inhibitor

Dai · J Cancer 2017

小分子 CHR-6494 占据 haspin 激酶的 ATP 口袋，阻断其对组蛋白 H3-Thr3 的磷酸化，使染色体无法正常排列与分离，把快速增殖的肿瘤细胞卡死在有丝分裂期并诱导凋亡。 The small molecule CHR-6494 occupies the ATP pocket of haspin kinase, blocking histone H3-Thr3 phosphorylation; chromosomes fail to align and segregate, trapping fast-dividing tumor cells in mitosis and driving apoptosis.

领域运用：Applications: 抗黑色素瘤候选药物，与 MEK 抑制剂联用协同增效、有望降低耐药；有丝分裂激酶（haspin、Aurora、PLK）整体上是肿瘤靶向治疗的重要靶点家族。 An anti-melanoma candidate that synergizes with MEK inhibitors and may delay resistance; mitotic kinases as a family (haspin, Aurora, PLK) are major targets of cancer therapy.
