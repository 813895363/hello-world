(Wuxi, China), managing 8 concurrent clinical trial projects across their full lifecycle — from pre-startup preparation (service requirements, lab timelines), document authoring & management (central laboratory operations manuals), and site initiation & training, to sample reception & testing, data transfer, and final close-out & archiving. I also provide Chinese-English translation support between internal lab teams and overseas sponsors' scientific teams, keeping overall management compliant and strictly aligned with GCP.

此前在美国麦迪逊 LabCorp Early Drug Development 工作三年，平均每年并行支持至少 7 个研究、累计参与约 40 个研究，所有研究活动均在 GLP 规范下执行；熟悉 CRO 协作模式、项目时间线跟踪与偏差（Deviation）管理，参与 Cell & Gene Therapy 及多项肿瘤相关药物研发项目，具备 SOP 合规与多物种动物模型研究的一线经验。 Previously spent three years at LabCorp Early Drug Development (Madison, WI, USA), supporting 7+ parallel studies per year and ~40 studies in total — all conducted under GLP. Deeply familiar with CRO collaboration, timeline tracking, and deviation management, with hands-on experience in Cell & Gene Therapy and oncology programs, SOP compliance, and multi-species in vivo research.

贯穿中美两段经历，我积累了以合规为底线的项目管理与实验执行经验——临床试验阶段的 GCP、早期研发阶段的 GLP，质量与合规始终是我推进每一个项目的前提。 Across my U.S. and China roles I have built compliance-first experience in both project management and study execution — GCP for clinical trials and GLP for early development. Quality and compliance are always the premise of how I run every project.
