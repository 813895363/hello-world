以 FDA 标准为核心：纵向从临床实验室 PM 延伸至医药研发全流程掌舵人，横向在总监级展开药企 / CRO / CDMO 三种组织形态的可选路线 Built on FDA standards as capability anchors: vertically from clinical-lab PM to helming the entire R&D pipeline, horizontally branching at director level across pharma, CRO and CDMO

主线 · 起步段MAIN TRACK · FIRST STOPS

目标：逐站吃透医药研发全流程，最终成为统筹全流程的研发掌舵人——整个医药研发中最重要的角色。每一站以对应 FDA 阶段的标准（GLP / GCP / GMP / IND / NDA）作为核心能力背书。 The goal: master every stage of the pipeline and ultimately helm the entire R&D process — the most pivotal role in drug development. Each stop is anchored to the FDA standard of its stage (GLP / GCP / GMP / IND / NDA).

三路线分岔 · 横向扩展THREE ROUTES · HORIZONTAL EXPANSION

同一能力底座，三种组织形态任选：药企深扎管线与临床开发策略，CRO 历练多申办方、多项目的规模化交付，CDMO 补齐 CMC 与 GMP 生产端——三者互为补充，都是通往全流程的完整拼图。 Same foundation, three organization types: pharma for pipeline & clinical development strategy, CRO for multi-sponsor delivery at scale, CDMO for the CMC & GMP manufacturing end — complementary pieces of the full-pipeline puzzle.

汇聚之后 · 通向全流程掌舵人AFTER CONVERGENCE

无论走哪条路线，2036 年前后都带着"研发 + 临床 + 生产"的复合经验汇入主线上行段——跨组织形态的阅历正是统筹全流程的底气。 Whichever route is taken, by ~2036 the combined discovery-clinical-manufacturing experience merges back onto the main track — cross-organization seasoning is exactly what helming the full pipeline demands.
