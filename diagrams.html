端粒结构：T-loop 与 ShelterinTELOMERE STRUCTURE: T-LOOP & SHELTERIN

染色体末端由数千个 TTAGGG 重复序列构成，末端回折形成 T-loop"套索"，3′ 单链尾侵入双链形成 D-loop，把脆弱的 DNA 末端藏起来，避免被误认为断裂。六种 shelterin 蛋白（TRF1、TRF2、RAP1、TIN2、TPP1、POT1）共同组装并维持这一结构——正是 Griffith 实验室电镜下直接观察到的图景。 A chromosome end carries thousands of TTAGGG repeats. The end folds back into a T-loop "lariat," and the 3′ single-stranded tail invades the duplex to form a D-loop, hiding the vulnerable DNA end from being mistaken for a break. Six shelterin proteins (TRF1, TRF2, RAP1, TIN2, TPP1, POT1) assemble and maintain this architecture — the very picture the Griffith lab observes directly under the electron microscope.

解读：How to read it: TRF1/TRF2 二聚体锚定在双链重复区并招募 TIN2–TPP1–POT1 桥联到单链尾；RAP1 依附 TRF2。T-loop/D-loop 结构一旦被破坏，DNA 损伤修复通路（如 Ramsden 研究的 Pol θ/TMEJ）就会错误地"修复"染色体末端，造成基因组不稳定——这把"方向"页的两条主线连在了一起。 TRF1/TRF2 dimers anchor the duplex repeats and recruit the TIN2–TPP1–POT1 bridge to the single-stranded tail; RAP1 rides on TRF2. Once the T-loop/D-loop is disrupted, DNA-damage pathways (such as the Pol θ/TMEJ studied by Ramsden) mistakenly "repair" the chromosome end, causing genome instability — connecting the two threads of the Interests page.

端粒酶延伸与 TERRA R-loopTELOMERASE EXTENSION & TERRA R-LOOP

端粒酶（TERT 催化亚基 + TERC RNA 模板）以自身 RNA 为模板，把 TTAGGG 重复逐个加到 3′ G-尾上——这是"持续合成能力（processivity）"的分子基础，也是抗衰老/永生研究的核心开关。同时，端粒转录出的 TERRA 长链非编码 RNA 会入侵双链形成 R-loop，既是端粒稳态的调控旋钮，失控时也成为基因组不稳定的来源（Griffith 方向）。 Telomerase (the TERT catalytic subunit plus the TERC RNA template) uses its own RNA to add TTAGGG repeats one by one onto the 3′ G-tail — the molecular basis of "processivity" and the core switch of anti-aging/immortality research. Meanwhile, telomere-transcribed TERRA lncRNA invades the duplex to form R-loops: a regulatory dial of telomere homeostasis and, when uncontrolled, a source of genome instability (the Griffith direction).

解读：How to read it: TERC 模板与 3′ G-尾配对，TERT 每轮转一次模板就加一个 TTAGGG——"轮转次数"就是 processivity，是我想通过 R-loop 相关酶的调控去提升的能力；Henikoff 的 CUT&RUN/CUT&Tag 则可用来定位调控端粒延伸的转录因子结合位点。 The TERC template pairs with the 3′ G-tail, and each template translocation by TERT adds one TTAGGG — the "number of rounds" is processivity, the capacity I hope to enhance by regulating R-loop-related enzymes; Henikoff's CUT&RUN/CUT&Tag can map where telomere-elongation transcription factors bind.

蛋白质设计与编辑循环THE PROTEIN DESIGN & EDITING CYCLE

现代蛋白质工程是一个"设计–构建–测试–学习"的飞轮：计算设计（Kuhlman 式从头设计）给出候选序列 → 基因合成构建饱和突变文库 → 表达纯化 → 高通量筛选（Fowler 式 DMS 给每个突变打分）→ 测序数据反哺机器学习模型，进入下一轮。每一轮都让蛋白更接近目标功能——抑制致癌 G 蛋白、做抗原门控的 PAGER 细胞（Kalogriopoulos），或我真正关心的：改造长寿相关蛋白。 Modern protein engineering is a design–build–test–learn flywheel: computational design (Kuhlman-style de novo) proposes candidates → gene synthesis builds saturated libraries → expression → high-throughput screening (Fowler-style DMS scoring every mutation) → sequencing data trains ML models for the next round. Each cycle moves the protein closer to its target function — inhibiting oncogenic G proteins, building antigen-gated PAGER cells (Kalogriopoulos), or what I truly care about: engineering longevity-related proteins.

解读：How to read it: 循环没有起点也没有终点——DMS 数据训练的模型让下一轮设计更聪明，结构验证把失败原因变成下一轮约束。左下角橙色框是这个飞轮对我个人的意义：用同样的范式改造端粒酶辅助因子与长寿相关蛋白，与基因治疗一起构成"永生"研究的工程底座。 The cycle has no true start or end — DMS-trained models make the next design smarter, and structural verification turns failures into next-round constraints. The orange box is what this flywheel means to me: applying the same paradigm to telomerase cofactors and longevity-related proteins, forming — together with gene therapy — the engineering base of immortality research.
